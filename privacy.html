Privacy

Effective November 21, 2026. Street Sharkz Professional Corporation, a New Jersey corporation.

This policy is between you and Street Sharkz Professional Corporation, a New Jersey corporation ("we," "us," "the Company"), whose office is in Camden, New Jersey. It covers the public site, the free assessment, the Crew Deck, the Members Portal, the App, and purchases. Questions go to Office@StreetSharkz.com or +1 862-310-0775.

What we collect

On the free assessment we collect your name, email, phone, delivery choice (email, text message, or both), your twelve answers, and the pillar scores the page computes.

On the Crew Deck join form we collect your name, crew alias, industry, stated purpose, email, whether you agreed you are 18 or older, whether you opted in to texts, and, only if you type one, a business or professional phone line.

When you ask for a Members Portal code we collect the email you enter and the 6-digit code we send. Do not forward the code. In the App we keep your profile (your pirate name and your shark’s name), your daily logs — for the Pup Deck, water and mindfulness minutes; for premium, all five pillars — and your dive-day count, so the App can run your plan and your rank.

If you post on the founders' floor we keep the text, the member name on the post, and the time. Other members in that room can read the post. Your email and phone are not shown there.

When you buy, our payment processor collects the card details and checkout fields. We do not receive or store your full card number. The processor sends us the name, email, what you bought, and the amount. A Founding Member order may also include a shipping address, used only to send the bracelet.

Why we use it

We use this information to send the assessment the way you asked, to run the Crew Deck and the Members Portal, to deliver what you bought, to answer you, to keep ordinary business records, and to meet the law. A business or professional line is not used for marketing blasts. Text messages go out only when you check the separate opt-in.

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell your personal information. Prospect packs are built from public business information. Your private details are not a product.

Who else receives it

We share personal information only with service providers who do a job for us, and only what that job needs, or when the law requires it. Our payment processor handles checkout. Email and text providers deliver the assessment report and the sign-in code. Our hosting provider serves the pages. If you follow a link off this site, that other site has its own rules, and clicking the link does not send them your email.

How long we keep it

We keep assessment answers, crew records, orders, and member posts for as long as we need them to deliver what you asked for, to keep the books, and to meet the law. A paid order may have to stay for tax and accounting even after a deletion request. A crew pass stored in this browser lasts only until it expires or you leave the deck.

Security

We use ordinary care to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and we do not guarantee absolute security.

What this browser stores

After you confirm a Crew Deck code, this browser's local storage holds a crew pass, your alias, and an expiry, for up to 30 days. Leave the deck clears it. After you confirm a Members Portal code, this browser's session storage holds the sign-in pass and a short profile (name, username, tier, and seat) for that tab. Sign out clears it. The pass is not written on the page. We do not use that storage to advertise to you, and we do not knowingly set advertising cookies.

Your choices

You may ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by writing to Office@StreetSharkz.com. Marketing email includes a way to opt out. If you receive texts, you may reply STOP to end them. Message and data rates may apply. We will honor a deletion request except where we must keep a record, such as a paid order.

Children

The site, the Crew Deck, the portal, and purchases are for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

If this policy changes, the new version posts on this page with a new effective date.
