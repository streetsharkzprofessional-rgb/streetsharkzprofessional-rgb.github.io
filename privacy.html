Privacy

Draft, pending counsel review. Effective November 18, 2026. Street Sharkz Professional Corporation, Camden, New Jersey.

We are Street Sharkz Professional. This page says what we collect, why, and how to ask us to delete it. Questions go to Office@StreetSharkz.com or +1 862-310-0775.

What you give us

On the free assessment we keep your name, email, phone, delivery choice (email, text message, or both), your twelve answers, and the pillar scores the page computes. We use that to show your map and to send the report the way you asked.

When you enter the Members Portal we email you a 6-digit code. The code is the key for that sign-in. Do not forward it.

If you post on the founders' floor, we keep the text of the post, the member name on it, and the time. Other members in the room can read it. Your email and phone are not shown there.

Purchases and the bracelet

Payments run through Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number. Stripe sends us the name, email, and what you bought. A Founding Member order also includes a shipping address, used only to send the glow-in-the-dark bracelet.

What the browser holds

After you confirm a code, this site stores the sign-in pass and a short profile (name, username, tier, seat) in the browser's session storage for that tab. The pass is not written on the page. Sign out clears it. We do not use that storage to advertise to you.

Who else touches it

Stripe processes the payment. If you open a public clip or the channel, that visit is on YouTube. We do not embed their player, and we do not send them your email. Email and text providers deliver the assessment report and the sign-in code. The site is hosted so the pages can load. Each of them gets only what the job needs.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal information. Lead Engine packs are built from public business information. Your private details are not a product.

How long we keep it, and deletion

We keep assessment answers, orders, and member posts for as long as we need them to deliver what you asked for, to keep the books, and to meet the law. You may ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to Office@StreetSharkz.com. We will respond. Some records, such as a paid order, may have to stay for tax and accounting even after a deletion request.

Age

The site, the portal, and the doors are for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new draft posts on this page with a new date.
