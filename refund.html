Refunds

Effective November 21, 2026. Street Sharkz Professional Corporation, a New Jersey corporation.

This page is the refund rule of Street Sharkz Professional Corporation, a New Jersey corporation ("Company," "we," "us"). It is part of the terms. "App" means the Sharked Up Peak Performance app — the free Pup Deck (hydration and mindfulness basics) for verified crew members who have taken the free assessment, and premium Peak Performance access, including the $10 beta and the beta access included with the $30 bundle or the $50 founding seat. "Prospect packs" means Starter at $50, Exclusive at $100, and Prospect Plus at $150. "Services" means work we do for you other than delivery of the App or a prospect pack, including a systems diagnostic, coaching, and a founding seat as membership.

The free doors

The free assessment, crew membership, and the Pup Deck are free. There is no charge, so there is nothing to refund.

What is not refundable

All Services are non-refundable. A purchase that mixes Services with the App does not make the Services refundable. Only the App, and only a prospect pack, can be considered, and only under this page.

What is refundable

The App and prospect packs are refundable up to 7 days after purchase, for a valid reason, and only if the Company approves the request. Sending a request is not itself an approval.

What a valid reason is

A valid reason is decided by the Company in its sole discretion. The examples are a duplicate charge, or non-delivery or an access failure that the Company does not fix after written notice. They are examples, not a complete list, and they do not limit that discretion. A change of mind is not a valid reason. Dissatisfaction, a prospect who does not buy, and results that differ from a testimonial are not a valid reason.

When an approved refund is paid

Approved refunds are processed within 90 days after the Company approves the request. A refund, if approved, goes back through our payment processor to the original payment method when that method can still receive it.

How to request a refund

The request must be in writing to Office@StreetSharkz.com, within 7 days after purchase, from the email on the order. Include the order details: what you bought, the date of purchase, the email on the order, and the reason. A request that arrives after the 7 days is late. A request that leaves out those details is incomplete. A late or incomplete request does not extend the 7 days.
