⚓︎ Vision & mission

A new model of therapeutic support.

Vision — We believe healing begins with feeling safe, understood, and supported—not simply diagnosed.

Too many people fall through the gaps between “I’m fine” and “I need clinical treatment.” We exist in that space, offering grounded support without medical gatekeeping.

Mission — Create spaces where people can reconnect with themselves through grounded human connection.

We anchor first—establishing safety and regulation—then help you find your arc forward through coaching, community, and practical tools.

⚓︎ Clear scope

What Anchor & Arc does not offer

Not a crisis service

01

If you or someone else is in danger, call 911. For a mental-health crisis, call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741.

Not a replacement for clinical care

02

We’re not a crisis service or a replacement for clinical care — and not a replacement for clinical treatment when diagnosis is needed. When clinical care is the right call, we hand off warmly.

We don’t claim to heal

03

Grounded, not guru: we hold space. We don’t claim to heal.

No diagnosis, no treatment, no gatekeeping

04

No diagnoses, no treatment plans, no medical gatekeeping. Grounded support comes before clinical intervention — never instead of it when it is needed.
