03 The craft

Narrative control — inner and outer

Every crew member is read twice: once by the story running in their own head, and once by the story the room reads from them. The Deck exists to train both.

The inner narrative

The first story is the one you tell yourself about who you are and what your work means. Run the loop on it: observe, think, act, log, improve, repeat — finesse in practication. A shark stays sharked up by keeping that story honest, fed by receipts, not wishes.

The outer narrative

The room reads your story before you speak — how you arrive, how you present, whether your word and your work match. Perceived trust and perceived value are built in small proofs, stacked daily: show up when you said, dress like the word you want believed, and let the log speak.

04 The crew standard

How the crew carries the brand

The crew's image is a shared asset — every member's presentation raises or lowers the value of the name for everyone else. The standard is simple:

Be on time. The word is the bond. Keep receipts. Log the work, don't narrate it. Present clean. Dress and speak like the professional the story says you are. Everyone eats. Credit shared is value multiplied — equity over gold. Stay lawful. Lawful piracy only: we create and acquire, we never take.
